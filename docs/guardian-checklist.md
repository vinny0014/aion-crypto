# Guardian readiness checklist

AION Crypto is considered technically healthy for continued AdSense readiness when the recurring guard and the full browser preflight agree on the following public conditions:

- canonical home responds normally and does not show a client-side application error;
- six pillar explainers respond 200, render their expected headings, and avoid stale-cache behavior;
- trust/legal pages remain reachable;
- `ads.txt` serves the exact authorized publisher record;
- robots rules do not block the whole site;
- sitemap includes all six pillars;
- backend readiness and database status are healthy;
- release markers are available after the release-fingerprint rollout;
- full browser preflight confirms mobile/desktop layout, console/network health, Consent Mode and CLS.

This checklist is a technical readiness guard, not a guarantee of Google AdSense approval.
